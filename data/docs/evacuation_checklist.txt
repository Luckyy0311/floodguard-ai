Emergency Evacuation Checklist

Essential Items:
1. Important documents in waterproof bag
2. Mobile phone and charger
3. Power bank
4. Drinking water
5. Ready-to-eat food
6. Medicines and prescriptions
7. Torch and batteries
8. Emergency cash
9. Extra clothes
10. Whistle or emergency whistle

Before Leaving Home:
1. Switch off electricity at main switch if safe.
2. Close gas supply.
3. Lock doors and windows.
4. Inform family members or neighbors.
5. Move to designated evacuation route.

If Evacuation Is Ordered:
1. Do not delay.
2. Follow official routes.
3. Avoid flooded roads.
4. Help children, elderly people, and persons with disabilities.
5. Go to the nearest shelter or safe building.

If You Cannot Evacuate:
1. Move to the highest safe floor.
2. Avoid closed attics where rising water may trap you.
3. Signal for help using torch, whistle, or bright cloth.
4. Share live location with emergency contacts.
5. Keep phone battery conserved.
