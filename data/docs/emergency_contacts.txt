Emergency Contact Guidance

General Emergency:
- Use your local emergency number.
- Examples: 911 in the United States, 112 in many countries, 108 or 112 in India.

Flood Emergency:
- Contact local disaster management authority.
- Contact municipal flood control room if available.
- Contact fire and rescue services for rescue assistance.

Medical Emergency:
- Call ambulance services immediately.
- Provide exact location, landmark, and patient condition.

Family Safety Plan:
- Keep one out-of-city relative as emergency contact.
- All family members should know meeting points.
- Keep emergency numbers saved offline.

Important Details to Share During Emergency Call:
1. Name
2. Location or landmark
3. Number of people needing help
4. Medical conditions
5. Flood condition near building
6. Phone number with backup contact
